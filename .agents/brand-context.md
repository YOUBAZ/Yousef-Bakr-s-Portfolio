## Open conflicts (Phase 1 review)

1. **Audience split.** Q3 names a CTO hiring at FAANG or a corporate. The audience
   field names founders, recruiters and business owners buying websites. Leading
   with cost and speed is a freelancer pitch and works against the hiring goal.
   Recommendation: hiring managers and tech leads at large companies come first,
   founders second, and cost drops out of the public brand.
2. **Age-led framing.** "Started at 15, hired at 18" is a prodigy story that anchors
   the reader on "junior". Recommendation: lead with the shipped product and state
   time as years of practice, not age.
3. **"Junior / undergraduate"** is accurate and stays internal. Public assets
   describe the work, not the rank.
