02

Colour

  • One signal colour, about 5% of any composition. The Merge Y branch, section numbers and one key figure.
  • Signal is never body text on paper. Use the signal type token, which passes at 5.9:1.
  • Links are ink with an underline. The underline turns signal on hover.
  • No gradients, glows or glass. No pure black or pure white.
